Master of Science in Electrical Engineering from Ohio University.

## Academic Focus

Graduate study focused on advanced electrical engineering theory and application, with coursework spanning electrical systems, signal processing, controls, communications, computation, and engineering analysis.

The program expanded upon my undergraduate engineering technology background by providing deeper theoretical and analytical treatment of electrical engineering concepts.

## Electrical Engineering

Graduate coursework required application of mathematical and engineering methods to analyze increasingly complex electrical systems.

The curriculum emphasized understanding system behavior through analytical modeling, computation, and quantitative engineering analysis rather than relying solely on empirical approaches.

## Computational and Analytical Methods

Graduate study included computational methods used to analyze engineering problems and evaluate system behavior.

Coursework provided experience applying engineering mathematics, numerical methods, modeling, and computational tools to electrical engineering problems.

## Systems Perspective

The degree strengthened the ability to move between individual electrical engineering disciplines and complete system behavior.

Coursework in areas including electronics, communications, controls, and related electrical engineering subjects provided a broader technical foundation for later systems engineering work involving integrated electrical and automated systems.

## Graduate Engineering Development

The M.S. in Electrical Engineering represents a progression from my applied engineering technology education into graduate-level electrical engineering.

The combination of hands-on engineering technology experience and graduate analytical training provided a foundation for professional work involving electrical design, systems engineering, instrumentation, automated test equipment, and complex integrated systems.
