Bachelor of Science in Engineering Technology with a concentration in Electro-Mechanical Engineering Technology from Miami University.

## Academic Focus

The program combined electrical, electronic, mechanical, controls, instrumentation, and engineering technology coursework with laboratory-based application.

The electro-mechanical focus provided a multidisciplinary foundation for working with systems in which electrical, mechanical, control, and embedded elements interact.

## Engineering Technology

Coursework emphasized applying engineering principles to practical systems rather than treating electrical and mechanical disciplines as isolated subjects.

The curriculum included circuit analysis, electronics, instrumentation, controls, process systems, mathematics, physics, statistics, and other engineering technology subjects.

Laboratory and project-based coursework provided experience with measurement, system analysis, troubleshooting, design, and implementation.

## Research Experience

While completing the degree, I participated in applied engineering research involving Industrial Internet of Things technologies.

The research involved development and evaluation of wireless embedded sensor nodes for industrial equipment monitoring, incorporating custom electronics, environmental and vibration sensing, embedded systems, data acquisition, calibration, and LoRa wireless communication.

The research resulted in a peer-reviewed publication at IEEE IECON 2018.

## Engineering Development

The degree provided a foundation spanning electrical engineering technology, embedded systems, instrumentation, controls, automation, and multidisciplinary system integration.

This combination of formal coursework, laboratory work, and applied research established the technical foundation for subsequent engineering roles involving electrical systems, automated test equipment, controls, instrumentation, and systems engineering.
