Graduate Certificate in Electronic Navigation from Ohio University.

## Academic Focus

Graduate-level study focused on electronic navigation and the electrical engineering principles underlying navigation systems.

The certificate provided additional specialization alongside my Master of Science in Electrical Engineering, extending graduate study into navigation-related engineering topics.

## Engineering Application

The coursework provided additional experience applying electrical engineering concepts to systems in which sensing, computation, communications, and signal processing contribute to navigation and positioning functions.

The subject matter complemented the broader analytical foundation developed through the M.S. in Electrical Engineering.

## Graduate Engineering Development

The certificate represents additional graduate specialization within my electrical engineering education and strengthened my background in navigation-related electrical and electronic systems.

Together with the M.S. in Electrical Engineering, the certificate contributed to a graduate-level foundation applicable to systems engineering, electronic instrumentation, communications, and integrated electrical systems.
