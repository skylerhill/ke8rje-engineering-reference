Provided technical support for university customers, including computer hardware, software, account troubleshooting, installation and configuration, and technical documentation.

## Technical Support

I assisted university users with a range of computing and technology issues involving hardware, software, accounts, and system configuration.

Support required gathering information about reported problems, reproducing or isolating issues where possible, identifying potential causes, implementing appropriate corrective actions, and confirming successful resolution.

## Hardware and Software Troubleshooting

Technical issues required distinguishing between hardware failures, software problems, configuration errors, account-related issues, and other potential causes.

I applied systematic troubleshooting methods rather than relying solely on individual symptoms, developing an early foundation in the structured problem-solving approach that I later applied to engineering systems.

## Installation and Configuration

I supported installation and configuration of computer hardware and software within the university environment.

This work required following established procedures while recognizing and resolving configuration or compatibility issues encountered during implementation.

## Customer and Technical Communication

Supporting users required communicating technical information to people with a wide range of experience and familiarity with computing systems.

I translated technical problems and solutions into understandable instructions while gathering the information necessary to diagnose issues effectively.

This experience strengthened the connection between technical problem solving and communication: a solution is only useful when it can also be clearly explained and successfully implemented.

## Technical Documentation

I supported documentation associated with technical issues, configurations, procedures, and resolutions.

Maintaining useful technical records helped make recurring problems easier to diagnose and provided information that could be shared among support personnel.

## Professional Development

This position provided my first sustained professional experience in technical troubleshooting and user support.

The skills developed through hardware and software troubleshooting, configuration, documentation, and technical communication became foundational to later engineering work involving electrical systems, automated equipment, instrumentation, software, and multidisciplinary system integration.
