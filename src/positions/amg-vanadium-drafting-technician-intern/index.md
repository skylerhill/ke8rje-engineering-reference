Supported engineering and manufacturing activities through CAD drafting, plant documentation, field verification, drawing revision, and organization of engineering information within an industrial manufacturing environment.

## Engineering Drafting

My work involved creating and revising technical drawings used to document plant equipment, utilities, and infrastructure.

AutoCAD was used to translate engineering information and field conditions into drawings that could be used by engineering, maintenance, and manufacturing personnel.

The work required attention to drawing accuracy, dimensional and spatial relationships, existing documentation practices, and consistency between engineering records and the physical facility.

## Field and Existing-System Documentation

A portion of the work involved documenting existing plant conditions rather than developing entirely new designs.

This required interpreting existing drawings and records, gathering information about installed equipment and infrastructure, and incorporating updated information into engineering documentation.

Working with existing systems provided early experience with a recurring engineering challenge: documentation does not always completely represent the current physical configuration, and discrepancies must be investigated before records can be confidently updated.

## Engineering Documentation

I supported the organization and maintenance of technical information associated with plant equipment and infrastructure.

This included drawing revisions and consolidation of engineering records to improve the accessibility and usefulness of existing documentation.

The work demonstrated the importance of engineering documentation as part of the lifecycle of an industrial facility, particularly for maintenance, troubleshooting, modification, and future engineering activities.

## Configuration Management

Drawing and record updates required maintaining identifiable and controlled engineering information.

This introduced practical configuration-management concepts, including revision control, consistency between related records, and preservation of the relationship between technical documentation and the installed physical system.

## Engineering Responsibilities

My responsibilities included AutoCAD drafting, drawing revision, interpretation of existing engineering information, field documentation, utility mapping, organization of technical records, and support of engineering documentation and configuration activities.

The position provided early professional experience applying engineering technology skills within an operating industrial facility and established a foundation in CAD, technical documentation, existing-system analysis, and configuration-controlled engineering information.
