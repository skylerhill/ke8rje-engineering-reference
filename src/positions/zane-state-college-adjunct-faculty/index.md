Served as adjunct faculty in Electrical and Electronic Engineering Technology, providing classroom and laboratory instruction in applied electrical and engineering technology subjects.

## Classroom Instruction

I taught engineering technology coursework with an emphasis on connecting electrical theory to practical engineering applications.

Instruction included electrical and electronic concepts, circuit analysis, measurement, troubleshooting, and the operation of electrical and electromechanical systems.

## Laboratory Instruction

A significant portion of the role involved hands-on laboratory instruction.

I guided students through laboratory exercises involving electrical measurements, instrumentation, circuit construction, equipment operation, troubleshooting, and interpretation of experimental results.

Laboratory instruction emphasized safe work practices, systematic troubleshooting, proper use of test equipment, and understanding the relationship between theoretical calculations and measured system behavior.

## Student Support

I worked directly with students to develop practical problem-solving skills and reinforce fundamental engineering concepts through demonstrations, laboratory exercises, and individual technical assistance.

The adjunct role established the foundation for my later full-time faculty responsibilities at Zane State College, where my work expanded into broader curriculum development, laboratory modernization, program leadership, and accreditation activities.
