Systems engineer supporting the modernization of mission-critical electrical test equipment used to verify guidance and control systems. The role combines systems engineering with hands-on electrical engineering across the complete development lifecycle, from reconstruction of legacy equipment and requirements definition through detailed design, integration, troubleshooting, verification, and implementation.

## Role Overview

My work focuses on the modernization of legacy automated test equipment containing obsolete or difficult-to-maintain electrical hardware. These systems require preservation of existing functional and electrical behavior while transitioning to modern, supportable architectures.

The work spans both systems-level and circuit-level engineering. Responsibilities include understanding the behavior and interfaces of existing equipment, translating that information into engineering requirements, developing replacement designs, coordinating interfaces across disciplines, and demonstrating that the resulting systems satisfy their intended requirements.

## Systems Engineering

Systems engineering responsibilities include requirements development and traceability, interface definition, system architecture, model-based systems engineering, technical coordination, design reviews, configuration-controlled documentation, integration planning, and verification and validation.

Modernization projects frequently begin with incomplete or legacy documentation, requiring engineering analysis and reverse engineering to establish the behavior that the replacement system must preserve.

## Electrical Engineering

In addition to systems engineering responsibilities, I serve in an electrical engineering role supporting the detailed design and troubleshooting of the test equipment.

This work includes schematic development and review, circuit analysis, power and control design, signal conditioning, instrumentation and measurement, component evaluation and selection, grounding and shielding, EMI/EMC considerations, electrical safety, and troubleshooting of integrated hardware.

The combination of systems and electrical engineering allows design decisions to be evaluated at both the individual circuit level and in terms of their effect on the performance of the complete test system.

## Integration, Verification, and Troubleshooting

I support hardware integration and verification from initial subsystem testing through operation of the complete test station. This includes developing and evaluating test methods, analyzing measured results, investigating unexpected system behavior, and performing root-cause analysis when hardware does not perform as expected.

Troubleshooting often requires tracing behavior across electrical circuits, instrumentation, control hardware, software-controlled interfaces, cabling, grounding, and the equipment under test rather than evaluating any single component in isolation.

## Engineering Standards and Documentation

The modernization of electrical test equipment requires consideration of applicable military, aerospace, electrical-safety, and electromagnetic-compatibility requirements.

Engineering work is documented through requirements, schematics, analyses, procedures, verification evidence, engineering changes, and other configuration-controlled technical documentation used to support design reviews and implementation.

## Technical Leadership

The role requires multidisciplinary coordination among systems, electrical, mechanical, software, test, manufacturing, and other engineering functions. I contribute electrical subject-matter expertise to technical decisions while coordinating the interfaces and verification activities necessary to deliver the complete system.

The projects associated with this position document representative examples of this work, including modernization of gyro balancing, drift testing, and impedance measurement equipment.
