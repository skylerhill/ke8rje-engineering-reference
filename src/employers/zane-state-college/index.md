I taught Electrical and Electronic Engineering Technology at Zane State College across a progression of faculty roles spanning classroom and laboratory instruction, curriculum development, program leadership, and engineering technology accreditation.

## Engineering Technology Education

My work focused on applied electrical and electronic engineering education, combining engineering theory with hands-on laboratory experience.

Instruction covered subjects including DC and AC circuits, electronics, sensors and instrumentation, electrical machines and transformers, industrial controls, programmable logic controllers, robotics, physics laboratories, and hydraulic and pneumatic systems.

A major emphasis of my teaching was connecting analytical concepts to practical engineering hardware, troubleshooting, measurement, automation, and laboratory experimentation.

## Curriculum and Laboratory Development

Beyond classroom instruction, I developed and modernized engineering technology curriculum and laboratory experiences.

This work included course development, laboratory planning, equipment evaluation and selection, development of instructional materials, and adaptation of technical coursework for multiple delivery formats.

Representative efforts included modernization of robotics curriculum and laboratory resources, development of hybrid and virtual engineering technology instruction, and support of multidisciplinary capstone design projects.

## Program Leadership

As my responsibilities expanded, I became involved in broader program administration and technical leadership.

This included curriculum planning, coordination of engineering technology courses and laboratories, support and mentoring of students, evaluation of program resources, and coordination with faculty and institutional stakeholders.

## Accreditation

I contributed to accreditation activities for engineering technology programs, including work associated with ABET Engineering Technology Accreditation Commission criteria.

These efforts required evaluation and documentation of curriculum, student outcomes, assessment processes, laboratory resources, continuous improvement, and other evidence used to demonstrate that the programs satisfied applicable accreditation requirements.

## Applied Engineering Education

My time at Zane State combined engineering practice with engineering education. In addition to teaching established technical concepts, I designed laboratory experiences, evaluated engineering equipment, supported fabrication and automation projects, troubleshot electrical and electromechanical systems, and guided students through the engineering design process.
