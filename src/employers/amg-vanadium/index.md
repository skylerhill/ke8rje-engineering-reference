I worked at AMG Vanadium as a Drafting Technician Intern, supporting engineering documentation and CAD activities within an operating industrial manufacturing facility.

## Industrial Engineering Environment

The position provided experience working with engineering information associated with an existing industrial plant, where drawings and technical records support the continued operation, maintenance, modification, and troubleshooting of physical equipment and infrastructure.

Unlike coursework centered on newly developed systems, this environment required working with installed equipment, legacy documentation, field conditions, and engineering records accumulated throughout the lifecycle of the facility.

## Engineering Support

My work included CAD drafting, drawing revision, field documentation, utility mapping, and organization of technical information associated with plant equipment and infrastructure.

A significant part of the work involved translating information from existing records and physical plant conditions into engineering documentation that could be used by engineering, maintenance, and manufacturing personnel.

## Engineering Documentation

The experience demonstrated the importance of maintaining accurate and accessible technical records within an industrial facility.

Drawings and equipment information needed to remain consistent with the physical systems they represented, requiring attention to revision control, documentation quality, and configuration of engineering information.
