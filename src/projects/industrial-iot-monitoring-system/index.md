Development of wireless embedded sensor nodes for industrial equipment monitoring and troubleshooting. The system incorporated temperature, humidity, and vibration sensing, custom electronics, embedded software, LoRa wireless communication, calibration, and communication-reliability testing. The work contributed to an IEEE IECON 2018 publication.
