Engineering documentation and data-consolidation project involving the mapping of plant utilities, development and revision of CAD drawings, and organization of equipment and drawing records into a more accessible information system.

## Engineering Challenge

Accurate plant documentation is important for maintenance, modification, troubleshooting, and future engineering work. Utility and equipment information existed across drawings, field information, and other records, creating a need to consolidate and improve the accessibility of the available engineering documentation.

The project required translating information about existing plant infrastructure into organized technical records while maintaining consistency between physical systems, drawings, and associated equipment information.

## Utility Mapping

I supported documentation of underground and surface utilities throughout the facility.

Field information and existing records were interpreted and translated into engineering drawings representing the location and routing of plant infrastructure.

This work required careful attention to spatial relationships, existing drawing conventions, and consistency between observed conditions and the resulting CAD documentation.

## CAD and Engineering Drawings

AutoCAD was used to create, revise, and organize engineering drawings associated with the facility and its utility systems.

Drawing work included incorporating field information, updating existing documentation, and improving the usefulness of drawings as engineering and maintenance references.

The work reinforced the importance of accuracy, consistency, revision control, and clear technical communication within engineering drawings.

## Information Consolidation

In addition to CAD work, I assisted with consolidation of equipment, drawing, and related engineering information into a more searchable and organized system.

The objective was to make existing technical information easier to locate and associate with the corresponding plant equipment or infrastructure.

This required organizing records consistently and considering how engineers, maintenance personnel, and other users would retrieve information during future work.

## Configuration and Documentation Control

Changes to plant documentation needed to preserve the relationship between engineering records and the physical systems they represented.

The project therefore involved principles of configuration management, including maintaining identifiable records, controlling revisions, and ensuring that updated documentation remained useful as a reference for existing equipment and infrastructure.

## Engineering Responsibilities

My responsibilities included interpretation of field and legacy information, utility mapping, AutoCAD drafting, drawing revision, organization of engineering records, and consolidation of equipment and drawing information.

The project provided practical experience with the documentation infrastructure that supports engineering throughout the lifecycle of an industrial facility.
