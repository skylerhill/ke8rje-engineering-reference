Ohio University provided my graduate engineering education, including a Master of Science in Electrical Engineering and a Graduate Certificate in Electronic Navigation.

## Graduate Engineering Education

My graduate study combined advanced electrical engineering coursework with specialized study in navigation systems.

The M.S. program included coursework in embedded systems, control systems, inertial and satellite-based navigation, integrated navigation, communications, microwave engineering, optoelectronics, vehicle control, electronic navigation, computational methods, and engineering communication.

## Electronic Navigation

The Graduate Certificate in Electronic Navigation provided additional specialization in navigation-related electrical and electronic systems alongside the broader M.S. curriculum.

The coursework developed additional understanding of navigation systems and the interaction of sensing, computation, communications, control, and electronic systems within navigation applications.

## Engineering Development

Graduate study at Ohio University expanded upon my undergraduate engineering technology background by providing deeper theoretical and analytical electrical engineering education.
